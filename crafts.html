India is a vast country, and every district, neighbourhood and village has its own craft. Shubharambh brings them onto one platform so a small artisan can sell beyond their own town, and so a craft from one state can find a home in another.

Why handicrafts

Choose Indian MSME products over imports

When I use a product from Gujarat and send our local products to Gujarat, our economy stays in India and our sense of brotherhood grows. We also carry each other’s culture with us. A small maker cannot market on that scale, so Shubharambh does the distribution.

Saves the Indian economy

Money spent on a handmade diya or barni stays with an Indian family business instead of an overseas factory.

Strengthens MSMEs

Artisan workshops get a national market, fair prices and steady orders that help the craft survive.

Shares cultures

Every gift carries a story: where it was made, by whom, and why it looks the way it does.
